Both are good products with real followings, Breathwrk has 100+ exercises and a 4.8★ rating from 18,000 reviews; The Breathing App pairs a minimalist design with music by Moby. ## What you're actually paying a breathing app for

Breathing techniques themselves are free, they're in [our guides](https://reground.pro/techniques/box-breathing/), in the NHS website, everywhere. What you're paying any app for is pacing, reminders, and how it feels in the hand. A one-time price means the app earns that money once, honestly, instead of betting you'll forget to cancel.

### Which breathing app works offline?

Reground runs entirely on the device, with no account and no analytics, so every technique works in airplane mode. BreathSesh states that its box and 4-7-8 exercises work fully offline. Breathing exercises need no connection by nature; some apps just require an account anyway.

### Do I need an app for box breathing at all?

No. Box breathing is four equal counts in, hold, out, hold, and it's free to learn anywhere, including our own [free guide](https://reground.pro/techniques/box-breathing/). What an app sells you is pacing, a visual to follow, and how it feels in the hand mid-panic, not the technique itself.
